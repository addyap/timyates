How it works

Simple, and at your pace

No jargon and no pressure. You decide how fast we go, and nothing happens until you're comfortable with it.

  1. A first conversation

    We talk about where you are, what you want, and what's worrying you. In person or by video, in plain English.

  2. A clear recommendation

    I look at the whole picture and set out what I suggest, why, and what it costs, in writing, before you commit to anything.

  3. Ongoing support

    Lives change and so do the rules. I keep your plan under review and I'm on the end of the phone when you need me.

About me

An expatriate since 1994, and still enjoying it

  • BA (Hons) Geography, University of Liverpool
  • Postgraduate Diploma in Marketing (DipM), Member of the Chartered Institute of Marketing
  • Member of the Chartered Insurance Institute, Certificate in Financial Planning
  • Co-gérant, Spectrum International Mortgages SARL

Before financial services I spent my career in marketing and business development in the healthcare and pharmaceutical industry. That taught me to listen first and to explain complicated things simply, which turns out to be most of what good financial advice is.

I'm a founding partner of The Spectrum IFA Group, which advises English-speaking clients across Europe, and I run Spectrum International Mortgages, the group's mortgage brokerage. My work centres on assurance vie, tax-efficient wealth management, and finding the right mortgage for each property purchase in France, including asset-backed lending.

I live in Fréjus. When I'm not working you'll find me skiing in the Alps or diving in the Mediterranean.
