How I help

Your money, arranged for life in France

French tax, French banks and French paperwork follow their own logic. I've lived as an expatriate since 1994, so I know what it's like to work it out from the other side.

  1. 01Assurance vie and investmentsFrance's favourite savings wrapper, and often the most tax-efficient home for your savings once you live here. I help you choose, structure and keep it under review.→
  2. 02Mortgages in FranceThrough Spectrum International Mortgages, which I run, I find the right French mortgage for your purchase and guide you through a process that works differently from the UK.→
  3. 03Asset-backed mortgagesBorrow against an investment portfolio instead of selling it. Useful for larger purchases, and for buyers whose income doesn't fit a French bank's usual boxes.→
  4. 04Moving to FranceBecoming French tax resident changes how your savings, ISAs and pensions are taxed. The best time to plan is before you arrive.→
  5. 05UK pensions in FranceWhat happens to your pensions when you're French resident, how they're taxed, and what your options are for drawing them.→
  6. 06Protection and health coverWith colleagues across Spectrum, I can help with life insurance and private health cover alongside your other planning.→

How it works

Simple, and at your pace

No jargon and no pressure. You decide how fast we go, and nothing happens until you're comfortable with it.

  1. A first conversation

    We talk about where you are, what you want, and what's worrying you. In person or by video, in plain English.

  2. A clear recommendation

    I look at the whole picture and set out what I suggest, why, and what it costs, in writing, before you commit to anything.

  3. Ongoing support

    Lives change and so do the rules. I keep your plan under review and I'm on the end of the phone when you need me.
